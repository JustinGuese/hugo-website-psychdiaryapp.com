The PsychDiary Team consists of licensed mental health clinicians, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) practitioners, and machine learning researchers dedicated to bridging the weekly therapy gap. 

Grounded in peer-reviewed psychological science (including Beckian cognitive restructuring, Pennebaker expressive writing, and Lieberman affect labeling), our mission is to build sovereign, German-hosted, privacy-first mental health journaling tools that empower authentic emotional self-awareness.
