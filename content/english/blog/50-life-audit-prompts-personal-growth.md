Most people navigate life on autopilot. Days blur into weeks, quarters into years, propelled by the urgent demands of overflowing inboxes, domestic obligations, and societal expectations. Then, at unpredictable moments—during a late-night flight, on a quiet Sunday afternoon, or following an unexpected health scare—a haunting question surfaces:

*"Is this the life I actually intended to build, or merely the one that accumulated around me?"*

In executive coaching and clinical psychology, stepping back to take an honest, holistic inventory of your entire existence is known as a **Life Audit**. Unlike superficial New Year's resolutions or productivity hacks, a true psychological life audit is a structured, values-based inquiry designed to measure the congruence between your daily reality and your deepest core values.

Rooted in **Self-Determination Theory** (Deci & Ryan, 2000) and the **Values Bullseye** of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT; Hayes et al., 1999), this guide provides the theoretical framework, the **Life Domain Alignment Scorecard**, and **50 copy-paste journaling prompts across 7 life dimensions** to reset your life trajectory.

## The Psychological Framework: Autonomy, Competence, and Relatedness

Self-Determination Theory (SDT) posits that human psychological flourishing relies on satisfying three basic, universal psychological needs:

```
                  ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │    THE TRIAD OF HUMAN FLOURISHING      │
                  │         (Deci & Ryan, 2000)            │
                  └──────────────────┬─────────────────────┘
                                     │
         ┌───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────┐
         ▼                           ▼                           ▼
┌──────────────────┐        ┌──────────────────┐        ┌──────────────────┐
│     AUTONOMY     │        │    COMPETENCE    │        │   RELATEDNESS    │
│  Feeling in      │        │  Experiencing    │        │  Feeling deeply  │
│  control of your │        │  mastery, skill, │        │  connected, seen,│
│  destiny & time  │        │  & capability    │        │  & loved by peers│
└──────────────────┘        └──────────────────┘        └──────────────────┘
```

When any of these three pillars is starved, burnout, apathy, and existential dread follow. A comprehensive life audit illuminates where your life is chronically deficient so you can reallocate time and emotional capital with surgical precision.

## 50 Copy-Paste Life Audit Journaling Prompts

Allocate 60 to 90 minutes in a quiet environment, or tackle one domain each day over a single week.

### Domain 1: Career, Mastery & Professional Purpose (Prompts 1–7)
1. If your current job title, company prestige, and salary were removed, what fundamental skills or contributions remain that you take genuine pride in?
2. In what areas of your work do you feel high autonomy (freedom of choice), and where do you feel trapped in robotic compliance?
3. What is one professional project you spent months pursuing that, in hindsight, was fueled by external validation rather than intrinsic interest?
4. What knowledge or technical craft would you enthusiastically study for 10 hours a week if there were zero economic pressure to do so?
5. Describe the moment in your work history when you felt most competent, capable, and in the "flow" state. What elements made that possible?
6. What is the unspoken ceiling on your current career path? What would happen if you dared to shatter it?
7. Complete this sentence: *"If I were being radically honest with myself, my current job is draining my..."*

### Domain 2: Emotional Health, Self-Talk & Inner Peace (Prompts 8–15)
8. What is the prevailing emotional tone of your inner self-talk when nobody is looking? Is it that of a demanding tyrant, an anxious parent, or a supportive ally?
9. What unhealed emotional wound from the last two years are you still actively trying to outrun through constant busyness?
10. How do you respond when you feel sadness, grief, or vulnerability? Do you allow the wave to crest, or do you reach for numbing behaviors (screens, food, shopping)?
11. What is one cognitive distortion (e.g., all-or-nothing thinking, catastrophizing) that repeatedly hijacks your mental clarity?
12. If you were guaranteed that expressing your true feelings wouldn't lead to abandonment, what would you say to the person who hurt you most recently?
13. On a scale of 1 to 10, how safe does it feel to do absolutely nothing for an entire afternoon? Why does stillness provoke guilt?
14. What emotional boundary do you continually compromise out of a desperate need to keep the peace?
15. What does true, unshakeable psychological safety feel like in your physical body?

### Domain 3: Relationships, Intimacy & Community (Prompts 16–22)
16. Who are the three people you spend the most time with? After being in their presence, do you feel emotionally energized or drained?
17. What hard truth have you been avoiding telling your partner, close friend, or family member?
18. Where are you performing a false version of yourself to maintain membership in a social circle that no longer aligns with your growth?
19. How frequently do you allow yourself to be genuinely vulnerable with another human being without intellectualizing your pain?
20. What relationship in your life feels one-sided, where you act as the eternal therapist/fixer with zero reciprocity?
21. If you were to audit your social media interactions, what percentage of your relational energy is spent observing strangers rather than deepening real intimacy?
22. What does deep, unconditional romantic love look like to you in this current chapter of your maturity?

### Domain 4: Physical Vitality, Somatics & Biology (Prompts 23–29)
23. What physical warning signals is your body currently sending you (e.g., chronic shoulder knots, digestive tension, migraines, exhaustion) that you have chosen to ignore?
24. How would you characterize your relationship with sleep? Is your bedtime a peaceful wind-down or a frantic battle against procrastination?
25. What physical movement or sport makes you feel alive and joyful, rather than feeling like a punitive calorie-burning obligation?
26. How do the foods and substances you consume on an average Tuesday affect your mental clarity and emotional stability 3 hours later?
27. When was the last time you felt truly rested—not merely absent from work, but biologically rejuvenated?
28. What is one somatic habit (e.g., cold showers, daily brisk walks, breathwork) that consistently resets your nervous system?
29. Complete this sentence: *"My body has carried me through so much. What it desperately needs from me right now is..."*

### Domain 5: Financial Alignment & Relationship with Money (Prompts 30–36)
30. What was the unspoken emotional narrative about money in your childhood home (e.g., *"Money is scarce,"* *"Wealthy people are selfish,"* *"Money equals safety"*)?
31. How much of your current monthly spending is devoted to soothing emotional stress versus building genuine freedom and experiential joy?
32. What does the word "enough" mean to you in terms of net worth, income, and lifestyle? Have you defined a finish line, or does the goalpost constantly move?
33. What financial decision or debt causes you the most latent anxiety, and what is the single next micro-action to resolve it?
34. If your money were an explicit vote for the kind of world you wish to live in, what do your bank statements say you are voting for?
35. What is one financial luxury you spend money on that brings zero genuine fulfillment? What would happen if you redirected it?
36. What would financial security allow you to say *no* to?

### Domain 6: Play, Leisure, Creativity & The Soul (Prompts 37–43)
37. What hobby, craft, or artistic pursuit did you adore between the ages of 8 and 14 that you completely abandoned in adulthood?
38. What is something you would love to do purely for the joy of it, even if you remain totally mediocre at it forever?
39. When was the last time you experienced genuine, gut-busting laughter that brought tears to your eyes? Who were you with?
40. How much of your leisure time is "active leisure" (reading, making, playing instruments, hiking) versus "passive consumption" (scrolling, streaming)?
41. What creative itch has been tapping on the shoulder of your subconscious for the past six months?
42. If you were given a completely free, unstructured Saturday with zero responsibilities and infinite energy, how would you design it from sunrise to midnight?
43. How does your inner critic sabotage your creative play by demanding that everything be productive or monetizable?

### Domain 7: Physical Environment & Sacred Sanctuary (Prompts 44–50)
44. Look around your primary living space. Does your home feel like a grounding sanctuary of peace, or an unmanaged storage locker of chaotic clutter?
45. What physical object in your home holds heavy, negative emotional baggage that you are finally ready to discard or donate?
46. How does your immediate physical workspace impact your ability to focus and maintain calm? What one change would instantly elevate it?
47. What natural environment (mountains, ocean, dense forest, desert) makes your nervous system feel instantaneously at home? How often do you visit it?
48. In what city, town, or neighborhood does your spirit feel most aligned? Are you living where you truly belong?
49. What sensory details in your environment (lighting, scent, music, tactile textures) could you intentionally upgrade this weekend to cultivate peace?
50. Closing synthesis: *"Looking across these 50 questions, the single most courageous shift my future self is calling me to make is..."*

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should I conduct a full psychological life audit?
Most clinical psychologists recommend conducting a comprehensive life audit once or twice per year—ideally at the start of a new calendar year, on your birthday, or immediately following a major life transition (job change, relocation, breakup). You can review individual domains quarterly to maintain alignment.

### What should I do if my scores are low across almost all domains?
Do not attempt to overhaul all seven domains simultaneously; this triggers executive overload and paralysis. Select the **single domain** with the lowest score that, if improved by 20%, would generate the greatest positive ripple effect across your life (often sleep/vitality or emotional health). Focus your micro-habits exclusively there for 30 days.

### Can I share my life audit answers with my therapist?
Yes. Bringing your life audit scorecard and prompt reflections into a therapy or executive coaching session is extraordinarily high-leverage. It saves weeks of exploratory inquiry, allowing your clinician to immediately identify core misalignments and design targeted behavioral interventions.
